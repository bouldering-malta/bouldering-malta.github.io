Bouldering in Malta

Bouldering here is sparse and underdeveloped. A handful of areas are written up; many more hold real potential and have barely been touched.

First ascents appear only where a record already existed and nobody disputes it. Where that is not the case the line is listed without one, rather than guessed at.

This guide is meant to grow. Go and explore, develop new problems, and photograph the blocks well enough to draw a topo on — then send them in.
